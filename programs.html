Program 01 · By the cutting, not the label

Hay & forage

Hay is not a standard product. The same field produces different hay in a first and second cutting, and a wet season changes everything. We tell you which cutting a load is from and what it looks like, and we would rather lose a sale than send you something that is not what you asked for.

Program 02 · Numbers, not opinions

Forage analysis

For an easy keeper, a metabolic horse or a performance diet, guesswork is not good enough. We arrange analysis through an independent laboratory and give you the report as it comes. What to do with those numbers is a conversation for your vet or nutritionist, not for us.

Delivery & standing orders

Running out of hay on a Sunday is how people end up buying whatever is available at any price. A standing order sized to your actual consumption means the next load arrives before that happens, and we stack it where you want it.
