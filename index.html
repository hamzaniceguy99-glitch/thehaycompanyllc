How we work

A Florida supplier, not your vet

The Hay Company LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L22000192793, filed in 2022, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We sell forage. We are not veterinarians or nutritionists, and what to feed a particular animal — especially one that is metabolic, elderly or in hard work — is a question for your vet or a qualified nutritionist. We will happily get a load analysed so that conversation has real numbers in it. And no supplier can promise natural forage is perfectly free of weeds or foreign material, so check your bales as you feed.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes, and you should. We will open a bale for you. Anyone unwilling to do that is telling you something.

What cutting is it?

We will tell you, honestly. First and second cuttings differ a lot, and pretending otherwise just produces an unhappy customer and a returned load.

My horse is metabolic. What should I feed?

That is a question for your vet or an equine nutritionist, not for us. What we can do is get the hay tested so they have real numbers to work from.

Do you deliver and stack?

Yes. Delivery is quoted separately and up front, and we stack where you want it provided the truck can get in.

What if a load is not right?

Tell us straight away. Forage varies, and if it is not what you were shown we would rather sort it out than argue.

How should I store it?

Off the ground, under cover, with air moving. Hay that arrives in good condition still spoils in a damp, closed barn.

Running low?

Tell us what you feed and we will show you a bale. Free, with no obligation.
